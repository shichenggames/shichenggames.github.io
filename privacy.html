1. Information on your device

The App stores coloring progress, check-in records, favorites, tool balances, and preferences on your device. If you choose a profile image, the App uses the image selected through Android's system picker. We do not provide cloud synchronization of these local records.

Using a feature can also generate a usage event as described below. Keeping your progress locally does not mean that no analytics or diagnostic information leaves your device. Image files are not included in our gameplay analytics events.

If you save or share an artwork, Android stores or sends the image to the location or app you choose. A destination service handles the shared image under its own privacy practices. The App does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.

2. Firebase analytics and crash diagnostics

Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand feature use and improve the App. Data can include app-instance identifiers, device and app information, session activity, and approximate region derived from network information. Our events describe actions such as opening a screen, selecting an artwork, coloring progress, using a tool, changing a setting, or claiming a check-in reward.

Google Analytics advertising ID collection is disabled in our App configuration. Analytics can still use its own app-instance identifier and other technical information; this setting does not disable identifiers used by separate services.

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and errors. Reports can contain error stack traces, app and operating-system versions, device characteristics, app-state information, installation identifiers, and recent activity relevant to a failure. Our diagnostic context includes the content version and build type. We do not set a real-name or email-based user ID for crash reports.

More information: Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

3. AppsFlyer attribution and measurement

We use AppsFlyer to measure installations, app sessions, referral sources where available, and selected in-app events. This helps us understand how people discover and use the App, check measurement quality, and detect fraudulent attribution.

Information processed can include an AppsFlyer-generated identifier, available advertising identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, language, timestamps, install-referral information, and interaction events. Technical diagnostics can include app-launch timing and device state, such as battery or network information. Network information can indicate a general region; the App does not request precise GPS location.

The integration disables Android ID collection in the AppsFlyer configuration. Other available identifiers, including advertising identifiers, are not disabled by that setting. The separate Google Analytics advertising ID setting described above does not disable AppsFlyer's identifier processing.

More information: AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy.

4. How information is used and disclosed

We use the information described above to provide App features, understand usage, diagnose technical problems, and measure installation sources. Google and AppsFlyer receive the information necessary to provide their respective services. Their service terms, privacy practices, and any applicable subprocessors also govern their handling of that information.

If you contact us, we use your email address and message to respond and handle your request. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to investigate misuse or protect rights and security.

This policy describes the Firebase and AppsFlyer integrations currently used by the App. If additional advertising or other services introduce new processing, we will update this policy to reflect that processing.

5. Website and external services

Our website is an informational site hosted on GitHub Pages. Some homepage images are loaded from Unsplash. Serving website pages and external images can expose technical request information, such as an IP address and browser details, to those providers. This is separate from the App's Firebase and AppsFlyer processing.

See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Unsplash Privacy Policy. When you follow another external link, the destination's privacy practices apply to your interaction with it.

6. Retention and deletion

Local App records remain on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall it, subject to the behavior of your device. Images exported to your gallery or shared with another service can remain there separately; you can remove them through that gallery or service.

We retain analytics, diagnostic, attribution, and support records only as needed for the purposes explained in this policy, taking account of service settings and applicable legal requirements. When those records are no longer needed, we use the relevant service's available controls to delete or de-identify them, subject to any required retention. We do not apply one retention period to every category.

Google states that Crashlytics normally keeps crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Analytics and AppsFlyer records follow their applicable retention settings and service processes.

Clearing or uninstalling the App does not itself erase information already sent to service providers. To request help with relevant records or ask about retention, contact us using the details below.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

  • You can clear local App data through Android's app settings. Doing so removes local progress and preferences.
  • Where supported by Android, you can manage, reset, or delete your advertising ID in device settings. This does not necessarily stop all analytics or attribution processing.
  • You can choose whether to select an image, save an artwork, share it, or send us a support message.
  • Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may request access, correction, deletion, or other available rights relating to information we control. Contact us to make a request.

The current App does not provide an in-app switch that turns off all analytics and attribution. We will assess requests under applicable requirements and may need information reasonably necessary to locate records and protect other people's privacy. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

8. Security and international processing

Connections to the analytics, diagnostic, and attribution services use encrypted transport. No storage or transmission method provides absolute security.

Our studio is based in Chengdu, China. The services we use may process information in countries or regions outside your own. The applicable service arrangements, hosting settings, and data-protection requirements govern that processing; selecting a reporting region does not mean all data is stored only in that region.

9. Children

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App or by contacting us, please notify us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.
